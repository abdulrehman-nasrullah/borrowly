How Borrowly Works

Three simple steps to borrow or lend items locally.

01

Find & Request

Browse listings across your city. Choose your required rental dates and send a dated request to the owner.

02

The owner reviews your dates and either accepts or rejects the request from their dashboard.

03

Meet & Pay Directly

Upon acceptance, both parties see each other's email to arrange pickup and settle the rental payment directly.
